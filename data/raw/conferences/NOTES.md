## Gaps

- Apr–Nov 2027 is thin: the city and Convention Center lists stop at Mar 2027, and many societies haven't named 2027 venues.
- Hotel conventions and corporate meetings are barely covered (no public calendars).
- Overseas counts are almost never published ahead.
- Small commercial "International Conference on …" series (icbbe, icsma, mlhmi, icccp, iccgv, iceim, dmip, ivsp, icbct, icget, iccia, iccmme) publish no numbers and may be low-quality events. They carry no attendees, so they add nothing to the city estimate.
